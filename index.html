Picture it first

Every question comes with a picture you can build. Draw the bars, jump along the line, group the dots — then answer.

Good mathematicians don't do sums blindly. They see the problem first. So each card below asks you to open its picture before the answer box unlocks. Play with it until the question makes sense, then type your answer. The questions never run out — just keep scrolling.

Remember: if a question feels confusing, draw it. A bar, a line, a few dots — the picture usually tells you whether to add, take away, multiply or divide.

Snap & Play
